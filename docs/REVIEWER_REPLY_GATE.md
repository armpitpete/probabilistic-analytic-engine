# Reviewer Reply Gate

## Technical preparation complete

The repository can now receive and screen genuine responses from the institutions contacted on 2 August 2026.

For each response, PAE can record:

- verified identity or stable identifier;
- affiliation and contact route;
- concrete expertise;
- proposed methods, domain, evidence or integrity role;
- case preparation or approval involvement;
- prior outcome exposure;
- source authorship and advisory relationships;
- financial, employment, personal, institutional, advocacy and political conflicts;
- willingness to authenticate a confidential review record;
- case-specific exclusions;
- the resulting eligibility state.

## Packet-release rule

A full case packet is released only when:

1. the candidate is eligible;
2. the selected case is not excluded;
3. the person did not prepare or approve that case;
4. source authorship and advisory conflicts have been checked;
5. no outcome material has been seen;
6. the declaration has been authenticated;
7. the packet is confirmed outcome-blinded;
8. the exact packet checksum and delivery record are fixed.

Institutional reputation, seniority, agreement with the project or willingness to help cannot override these gates.

## While waiting

The scheduled weekday mailbox check should identify:

- responses;
- referrals;
- declines;
- requests for information;
- potential conflicts;
- required follow-up actions.

One follow-up may be sent after seven working days without a response. Repeated reminders are not authorised unless the institution invited further contact.

## Current stop condition

No further repository implementation is required before a genuine reply arrives. The parent calibration programme remains open because real analyst assignments, independent reviews, authenticated findings and aggregate scoring cannot be created by software.

No live-case use, packet release, public conclusion, publication or deployment is authorised.
