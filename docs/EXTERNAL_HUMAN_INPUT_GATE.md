# PAE External Human Input Gate

## Completed technical programme

The repository has completed every reversible technical step required to begin the resolved-case calibration exercise:

- frozen and balanced corpus;
- cutoff and outcome-leakage controls;
- fixed acceptance thresholds;
- aggregate scoring;
- analyst packets;
- reviewer packets;
- role-separation checks;
- assignment register;
- invitation and declaration templates;
- schemas and deterministic validation.

## Why the programme stops here

Independent review is evidence about real people and real conduct. It cannot be manufactured by code, by an AI system or by assigning different internal labels to one decision-maker.

The following facts must be genuine and externally verifiable:

- who prepared each case;
- who approved the frozen inputs;
- who reviewed each case;
- whether reviewers were independent;
- whether conflicts were disclosed;
- whether outcome blinding was preserved;
- what reviewers actually found;
- whether each review was signed or otherwise authenticated.

## Minimum human structure

Each case requires:

- one preparer;
- one different approver;
- two external reviewers who did not prepare or approve that case.

The same reviewer may cover multiple cases if independence and workload remain credible. The same person must not act as preparer and approver for one case, and must not externally review a case they prepared or approved.

## Owner-supported actions

The project owner must either:

1. nominate suitable real people; or
2. authorise outreach to suitable candidates using the repository invitation template.

For every nominee, record:

- name or stable reviewer identifier;
- affiliation;
- relevant expertise;
- relationship to the project and case;
- known conflicts;
- whether outcome material has already been seen;
- willingness to sign the confidential review record.

## What happens after nominations

1. Populate the assignment register.
2. Validate role separation.
3. Deliver only outcome-blinded packets.
4. Freeze analyst assignments and reviews.
5. Reveal outcome records.
6. Build the result bundle.
7. Run aggregate calibration.
8. Apply the predeclared pass, correction or stop decision.
9. Seek explicit authority before any subsequent live-case programme.

## Prohibited shortcuts

Do not:

- invent names or reviewer identities;
- use AI-generated signatures;
- count internal role separation as external review;
- show reviewers the outcome labels before their reviews are frozen;
- replace a difficult case after seeing its result;
- adjust thresholds after scoring;
- interpret a pass candidate as automatic live-use authority.
