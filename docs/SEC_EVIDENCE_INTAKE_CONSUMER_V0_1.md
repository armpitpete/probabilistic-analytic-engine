# SEC Evidence Intake consumer v0.1

PAE may consume Story Evidence Collector Evidence Intake Handoff v1 only as **evidence acquisition input**.

The consumer validates producer identity, Evidence Pack validation/integrity, source identity/provenance, collection and preservation structures, dependency state, uncertainty, human review, explicit non-authorities and the PAE consumer profile.

A valid handoff does **not** mean the evidence is sufficient, true, independent enough for analysis or calibrated.

PAE still owns:

- competing-hypothesis construction;
- evidence-requirement design;
- dependency/independence assessment under the PAE case;
- information-sufficiency state;
- calibration gates;
- probability inputs and calculations;
- human review and any later publication decision.

The SEC handoff is rejected if it attempts to carry hypotheses, probabilities, likelihoods, attribution or information-sufficiency claims into PAE.

This compatibility surface performs no automatic evidence collection and does not authorise live unresolved-case analysis.
