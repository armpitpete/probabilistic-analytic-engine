# Reviewer Reply and Follow-Up Templates

## Interested individual — initial reply

**Subject:** PAE historical review — independence screening

Thank you for your interest in reviewing the Probabilistic Analytic Engine historical calibration exercise.

Before any case packet is sent, we need to confirm role suitability, independence and outcome blinding. Please provide:

- your current affiliation and relevant expertise;
- which role best fits: methods, migration/domain, evidence/provenance or research integrity;
- whether you prepared, approved or advised on any case in the programme;
- whether you authored or materially contributed to sources concerning any of the cases;
- whether you have already seen the later outcome records for the relevant case;
- any financial, employment, personal, institutional, advocacy or political conflict;
- whether you are willing to complete and authenticate a confidential review record.

No case packet or outcome information is included at this stage. Institutional affiliation does not itself establish eligibility.

## Institution offers to circulate

**Subject:** Re: PAE independent historical review invitation

Thank you for offering to circulate the invitation.

Please share it only with individuals who may have relevant methods, migration-policy, evidence-verification or research-integrity expertise. Interested people should respond directly so that independence, prior case exposure and conflicts can be assessed individually.

The institution is not being asked to endorse the project, and no person will be treated as eligible solely because of their affiliation.

## Eligible candidate — declaration stage

**Subject:** PAE historical review — declaration required

Your preliminary intake record has passed the general eligibility checks. Before a specific case is assigned, please complete the attached declaration confirming:

- no preparation or approval role in that case;
- no disqualifying authorship, advisory or party relationship;
- no prior access to the frozen outcome label or withheld outcome sources;
- complete conflict disclosure;
- willingness to authenticate the returned confidential review record.

The full outcome-blinded packet will be sent only after the declaration and case-specific exclusions are validated.

## Polite decline

**Subject:** PAE historical review invitation

Thank you for considering the request. We understand that you are unable to participate. No further action is needed, and no case material has been shared.

## No-response follow-up

**Subject:** Follow-up: independent outcome-blinded historical review

I am following up on the invitation sent on [date]. We are seeking volunteers or suitable referral routes for an outcome-blinded review of an auditable historical analysis method.

The request does not seek institutional endorsement. No live case is involved, and no case packet is sent before individual independence and conflict screening.

Please let us know whether the invitation can be circulated or whether another contact would be more appropriate. No reply is required if this falls outside your remit.

## Follow-up timing

- Send one follow-up after seven working days without a response.
- Do not send repeated reminders after that unless the institution invited further contact.
- Record replies, declines, referrals and non-responses in the project issue.
- Do not treat silence as rejection of the method or as evidence about reviewer availability.
