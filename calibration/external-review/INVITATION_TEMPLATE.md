# Invitation to Review a Probabilistic Analytic Engine Case

## Subject

Independent outcome-blinded review of a historical analytic case

## Invitation

We are testing the **Probabilistic Analytic Engine**, an auditable method for comparing competing explanations and recording uncertainty.

We are seeking independent review of one resolved historical case. The review is **outcome-blinded**: you will receive only information available by a predeclared historical cutoff. You will not receive the later outcome label or post-cutoff outcome sources until your signed review has been frozen.

The task is to assess whether:

- the question is precise and scoreable;
- the hypothesis classes are applied coherently;
- the evidence is inspectable and fairly represented;
- source dependencies and repeated reporting are handled correctly;
- contrary evidence and ordinary explanations have been sought;
- the probability assignment is reproducible and adequately justified;
- any hindsight information has entered the packet.

You are not being asked to endorse a political conclusion. You may accept the packet, accept it with minor comments, or reject it. Findings are classified as minor, major or critical.

## Independence requirements

Before receiving the full packet, please confirm that:

- you did not prepare or approve the case evidence or calculation;
- you have no relevant financial, employment, authorship or close personal conflict;
- you have not seen the frozen outcome label or withheld outcome sources;
- you will disclose relevant institutional, advocacy or political commitments;
- you are willing to identify your affiliation and relevant expertise in the confidential review record.

The review record may be retained as audit evidence. No public attribution will be made without separate permission.

## Expected return

Please return:

1. the completed reviewer declaration;
2. answers to the structured review questions;
3. any findings with severity and explanation;
4. an accept, accept-with-minor-comments or reject decision;
5. a dated signature or equivalent authenticated confirmation.

## Important limits

This programme does not authorise live-case use, automatic attribution or publication. External review tests the method and packet quality; it does not convert an institutional outcome into judicial proof.
