# External Review Package

## Reviewer role

Review the pre-cutoff evidence packet and the calculation record without accessing the frozen outcome label or post-cutoff outcome sources.

This is a method and reproducibility review. It is not a request to endorse a political conclusion.

## Required reviewers

Each case requires at least two real external reviewers.

Suitable coverage across the reviewer pool should include:

- structured analytic methods or probabilistic forecasting;
- source provenance and open-source intelligence;
- the relevant migration, border or regional domain;
- research ethics or reproducibility.

One person may review more than one case, but each case still requires two distinct reviewers.

## Before receiving a packet

The reviewer must complete the declaration form and confirm:

- no role in preparing the case, priors, likelihoods or calculation;
- no relevant financial, employment, authorship or close personal conflict;
- no prior access to the frozen outcome class or withheld outcome sources;
- disclosure of relevant advocacy, institutional and political commitments.

## Review questions

1. Is the question precise and scoreable?
2. Are the hypotheses collectively adequate and genuinely exclusive for the calculation?
3. Does every material evidence claim have inspectable provenance?
4. Are source dependencies and shared origins handled correctly?
5. Are important contrary sources or ordinary explanations missing?
6. Are priors and likelihood ranges explained well enough to dispute?
7. Can the calculation be reproduced from the supplied record?
8. Is any post-cutoff information visible or inferable?
9. Does the evidence confidence match the actual public record?
10. Should the packet be accepted, accepted with minor comments or rejected?

## Severity

- `minor` — wording, presentation or low-consequence clarification;
- `major` — reparable defect that could materially affect the result;
- `critical` — leakage, unauditable provenance, irreproducibility, missing central hypothesis, independence failure or another defect that invalidates the case.

## Freeze procedure

The signed review record must be frozen before the outcome file is revealed.

After freezing, the reviewer may inspect the outcome source and comment on whether the predeclared resolution standard was applied consistently. That second-stage comment must not alter the original blinded review.

## Independence limit

A repository field saying `independent: true` is evidence of a declaration, not proof of independence. The programme owner must verify reviewer identity and conflicts outside the calculation engine.
